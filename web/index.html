    What these numbers mean—and what they don’t

    Units authorized are housing units authorized by building permits. They are not completed homes, homes for sale, or occupied households. One building can authorize several units.

    Prior-year change compares the same county and annual basis. A missing or zero prior-year denominator is shown as unavailable. Large percentage changes in small counties can reflect small absolute differences.

    Construction valuation, where present in the downloaded data, is reported permit construction value in nominal U.S. dollars. It is not a property sale price or inflation-adjusted market value.

    Freshness distinguishes the source reporting period, retrieval date, and export date. An export generated this year does not make an earlier reporting year current. This platform is descriptive and does not predict future construction.
